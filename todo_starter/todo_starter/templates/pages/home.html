FROM BROWSER STORAGE TO YOUR ACCOUNT

What happens when you sign in?

When the signed-in todo list opens:

  1. The app reads any todos you created while signed out from IndexedDB.
  2. It adds them to your account in the server database.
  3. After the server confirms they were saved, it deletes this app’s IndexedDB database.

This is a one-time move, not ongoing synchronization between IndexedDB and the server.

If saving fails, your browser copy is kept so you can retry. Retrying does not create duplicate todos.

IMPLEMENTED BEHAVIOR

Signed out vs. signed in

Signed out: browser storage

  • Add, edit, complete, filter, and delete todos without an account.
  • All todo data is stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. Todo data is not sent to the server.
  • The list remains when you close and reopen the page in the same browser on the same device.
  • Other browsers and devices cannot access it.
  • Clearing this site’s browser data deletes the list.

Signed in: server storage

  • The same todo features save to the server database under your account.
  • Your todos are available on other devices when you sign in to the same account.
  • Refresh the list to see changes made on another device.
  • Existing signed-out todos are moved from IndexedDB to your account.
  • New signed-in todos are saved to the server, not IndexedDB.
  • Signing out does not copy account todos back to the browser. The signed-out list starts empty.

Accounts and Google integration

  • Create an account and sign in with email and password, or use Google sign-in.
  • After signing in, open Integrations to connect Google Calendar separately.
  • The integration displays the next 10 events from your primary Google Calendar.
  • Calendar access is read-only. Todos are not synced with Google Calendar.
  • Google sign-in and Calendar require OAuth credentials to be configured by the person running this project.
